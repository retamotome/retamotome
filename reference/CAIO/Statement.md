## Rationale for Adopting Natal Chart Analysis | 採用星盤分析之理由  

This report utilizes Western natal chart analysis as a framework for exploring personality characteristics, cognitive tendencies, and potential development directions. The primary objective is not to predict future achievements, nor to serve as a basis for recruitment, promotion, or performance evaluation. Instead, it seeks to provide a relatively stable archetypal model that is less influenced by temporary circumstances and can assist in identifying potential cognitive strengths, areas of interest, and long-term developmental potential.  
本報告採用西洋星盤作為人格特質、認知傾向與潛能方向之分析框架，其主要目的並非預測個人未來成就，亦非作為任用、升遷或績效評核之依據，而是希望透過一套相對穩定且不易受到當下情境影響的人格原型模型，協助探索個體可能具備的認知優勢、興趣傾向及長期發展潛力。  

Within the field of talent assessment, tools such as MBTI, DISC, and other personality assessment frameworks are fundamentally designed to describe individual preferences, traits, and behavioral tendencies through specific theoretical structures and statistical observations. However, these methods share a common limitation in that assessment outcomes may be influenced by temporary emotional states, self-awareness levels, role expectations, organizational culture, and working environments. As a result, even the same individual may demonstrate significant variations in behavior or questionnaire responses at different points in time.  
在人才評估領域中，無論是 MBTI、DISC 或其他人格測評工具，本質上皆透過特定理論架構與統計歸納方法，嘗試描述個體的偏好、特質及行為傾向。然而，多數人格評量工具同樣面臨共同限制，即受測結果可能受到個人當下情緒、自我認知程度、角色期待、組織文化及實際工作環境等因素影響。因此，即使是相同個體，在不同時期所呈現的行為與回答內容，也可能存在顯著差異。  
 
For this reason, the report adopts natal chart analysis not because it is considered inherently more accurate than conventional personality assessment models, but because it offers an alternative perspective for examining personality structures and cognitive patterns. From the perspective of this report, the value of natal chart analysis lies not in deterministic prediction, but in its ability to generate reasonable hypotheses regarding an individual's potential, interests, and professional characteristics, thereby providing additional insight for talent development and role allocation decisions.  
基於上述考量，本報告選擇運用星盤作為分析工具，並非主張其具有高於市場主流人格模型的絕對準確性，而是認為其同樣能夠提供一種觀察人格結構與認知模式的視角。從本報告的觀點而言，星盤的價值不在於提供確定性的預測結果，而在於協助形成關於個人潛能、興趣方向及職能特質的合理假設，進而作為組織進行人才發展與職務配置時的參考資訊。  

## Distinction Between Potential and Performance | 潛能與績效之區別  
This report makes a clear distinction between the concepts of Potential and Performance. Potential reflects the capabilities, cognitive strengths, and learning tendencies that an individual is more likely to develop over time, whereas performance reflects the actual outcomes achieved within a specific role, environment, and period. Although related, these concepts are not identical. Individuals with strong potential may not immediately demonstrate exceptional performance in every circumstance, while individuals with outstanding current performance may not necessarily be suitable for all future roles or career paths.   
本報告特別區分「潛能（Potential）」與「績效（Performance）」兩項概念。潛能反映的是個體可能較容易發展的能力方向、認知優勢及學習傾向，而績效則反映個體在特定職務、特定環境與特定時間點下所呈現的實際成果。兩者雖然相關，但並不等同。一位具備高度潛能的人才，未必能在所有情境中立即展現卓越績效；同樣地，一位目前績效優異的人員，也未必適合所有未來角色與發展方向。  

Accordingly, natal chart analysis is positioned within this report as a tool for talent exploration and potential identification rather than a performance prediction mechanism. Its purpose is to help organizations better understand an individual's personality traits, thinking patterns, interests, and developmental potential from multiple perspectives, thereby supporting more informed talent development and role assignment decisions.   
因此，本報告將星盤定位為人才探索與潛能辨識工具，而非績效預測工具。其主要功能在於協助組織從不同角度領會個體可能具備的人格特質、思維模式、興趣方向及職涯發展潛力，使組織在人才培育與職務安排上能夠擁有更完整且更多元的觀察視角。  
  
## Importance of Performance-Based Validation | 績效驗證的重要性  
The report further recognizes that conclusions derived from any potential assessment framework must ultimately be validated through actual work outcomes. Whether an individual is truly suited to a specific role should therefore be evaluated through objective measures such as KPIs, OKRs, project achievements, leadership effectiveness, and organizational contributions. Potential assessments provide directional hypotheses, while performance outcomes provide real-world validation; both should be regarded as complementary rather than mutually exclusive.   
本報告同時認為，任何潛能分析結論最終仍需透過實際工作成果加以驗證。因此，個體是否真正適合特定職務或角色，仍應透過 KPI、OKR、專案成果、領導表現及組織貢獻等客觀指標進行長期觀察與驗證。潛能分析所提供的是方向性的假設，而績效成果所提供的是現實層面的驗證，兩者應被視為相互補充而非相互取代。   

## Methodological Considerations and Limitations | 方法論考量與限制  
It is also important to recognize that even commonly accepted management tools such as KPIs and OKRs may be influenced by metric design, organizational incentives, departmental characteristics, and evaluation periods. Consequently, no talent assessment methodology can be considered entirely objective or free from bias. Personality models, psychological assessments, behavioral observations, managerial evaluations, and performance management systems should all be understood as different lenses through which an individual may be evaluated, each possessing its own strengths and limitations.   
值得注意的是，即使是 KPI 與 OKR 等廣泛被企業採用的管理工具，其本身亦可能受到指標設計方式、組織誘因、部門特性及評量期間等因素影響，因此並不存在絕對客觀且完全無偏差的人才評估方法。人格模型、心理測驗、行為觀察、主管評價及績效管理制度，本質上皆屬於從不同角度觀察個體的工具，各自具有其價值與限制。    

## Core Position and Application Principles | 核心立場與應用原則  
The core position of this report is that natal chart analysis should be viewed as a reference framework for exploring individual potential and personality archetypes, rather than as a tool for personnel decisions. Performance management should likewise be viewed as a mechanism for validating outcomes and organizational contributions, rather than as a means of assessing potential. When used together, these complementary perspectives enable organizations to evaluate talent through both a developmental and a performance lens. Such an approach not only helps identify capabilities and future potential that may not yet be recognized by conventional organizational processes, but also ensures that these potential attributes are validated through measurable achievements and real-world contributions. As a result, organizations are able to establish a more comprehensive foundation for talent development, career planning, and role assignment decisions.  
本報告的核心立場在於，星盤分析應被視為探索潛能與人格原型的參考工具，而非決策工具；績效管理則應被視為驗證成果與實際價值的評估工具，而非潛能判定工具。當兩者相互搭配時，組織能同時從潛力面與績效面識別人才，不僅有助於發掘尚未被看見的發展可能性，也能透過實際成果驗證其價值，進而形成更完整的人才發展與職務配置參考依據。
