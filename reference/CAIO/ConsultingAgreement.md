## 第四條　服務方式及顧問額度 | Article 4 Service Structure and Consulting Capacity

### 一、月度顧問服務安排 | Section 4.1 Monthly Retainer Services    

本契約採月度顧問費制，每月包含之服務額度為二十（20）小時。乙方支付之顧問費，係用於保留甲方之專業服務能力、研究投入及回應優先權。甲方原則上於一般工作時間內提供服務。非緊急事項之回應時間以二（2）個工作日內為原則。非經甲方同意，甲方無提供夜間、假日或國定假日服務之義務。  
The Services shall be provided on a monthly retainer basis and shall include up to twenty (20) consulting hours per calendar month on a non-rollover basis.  
The monthly consulting fee is intended to secure the Consultant's availability, consulting capacity, research efforts, and service priority.  
Unless otherwise agreed, the Consultant shall provide Services during normal business hours. For non-urgent matters, the Consultant will use commercially reasonable efforts to respond within two (2) business days.  
The Consultant shall have no obligation to provide services during evenings, weekends, or public holidays unless expressly agreed otherwise.  

### 二、顧問額度之性質 | Section 4.2 Nature of Consulting Capacity

前項服務額度並非固定出勤工時，而係作為雙方衡量顧問服務投入及超額服務之計算基準。  
The monthly consulting capacity is not intended to constitute fixed working hours or employment-related attendance requirements, but serves solely as a reference for measuring consulting effort and determining any additional services.  

### 三、服務額度涵蓋內容 | Section 4.3 Included Consulting Activities

服務額度包括但不限於：  
The consulting capacity may include, without limitation:  

（一）會議及即時技術諮詢；  
（a）Meetings and real-time consultations;  

（二）文件及技術資料審閱；  
（ｂ）Review of documents and technical materials;  

（三）顧問意見、分析報告及其他交付成果之準備；  
（ｃ）Preparation of recommendations, analyses, reports, and deliverables;  

（四）會後分析、整理及必要之後續研究；  
（ｄ）Post-meeting analysis and follow-up research;  

（五）技術資料蒐集、公開資訊研究及方案比較；  
（ｅ）Technology research, data gathering, and solution evaluation;  

（六）特定技術議題之分析、驗證及準備工作；及  
（ｆ）Investigation, validation, and preparation relating to specific technical subjects; and

### 六、額度不得累積 | Section 4.6 No Rollover of Unused Capacity    

除雙方另有書面約定外，當月未使用之服務額度不得自動累積至次月。
