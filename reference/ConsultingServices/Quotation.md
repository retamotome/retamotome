## 服務說明 | Service Overview  

本顧問服務旨在協助企業進行系統架構、技術方案及技術策略之分析與評估，提供客觀且專業之技術建議，以降低技術導入風險、提升系統整合效益，並支援企業之技術決策與長期發展規劃。  
The consulting services are intended to support organizations in evaluating system architectures, technical solutions, and technology strategies. Independent and professional recommendations will be provided to reduce implementation risks, improve system integration effectiveness, and support both technical decision-making and long-term technology planning.  

### 顧問服務範圍 | Scope of Consulting Services

本顧問服務範圍包含但不限於下列項目：  
The scope of consulting services includes, but is not limited to, the following:  

一、系統架構分析及評估  
a. System Architecture Analysis and Evaluation  

針對現有或規劃中之系統架構進行分析與評估，協助識別系統設計之優勢、限制及潛在風險。  
Evaluation of existing or proposed system architectures to identify strengths, limitations, architectural concerns, and potential risks.  

二、技術方案研究、比較及可行性分析  
b. Technical Solution Research, Comparison, and Feasibility Assessment  

依據業務需求與技術目標，研究並比較不同技術方案之適用性、可行性、導入成本及預期效益。  
Research and comparative assessment of alternative technical approaches, including feasibility, implementation considerations, estimated costs, and expected benefits.  

三、系統整合及技術風險分析  
c. System Integration Review and Technical Risk Analysis  

評估系統整合需求、介接架構及潛在技術風險，並提出改善建議與風險因應措施。    
Review system integration requirements, interface architecture, technical dependencies, and associated risks, together with mitigation recommendations.   

四、技術問題專業諮詢  
d. Professional Consultation on Technical Issues and Challenges  

針對技術問題、架構議題或系統設計挑戰提供專業意見與建議。  
Provide professional guidance and recommendations regarding technical issues, architectural concerns, and engineering challenges.  

五、新興技術、產品或解決方案研究與評估  
e. Evaluation of Emerging Technologies, Products, Platforms, and Solutions  

研究與評估新興技術、產品、平台或解決方案之應用價值、導入可行性及潛在影響。  
Assess emerging technologies, products, platforms, and solutions with respect to business value, technical feasibility, and potential impact.  

六、技術策略及長期系統演進方向建議  
f. Technology Strategy and Long-Term System Evolution Recommendations  

提供技術發展策略、系統演進路線圖及未來技術規劃建議，以支援企業長期發展目標。  
Provide recommendations for technology strategy, system evolution roadmaps, and future-state planning aligned with organizational objectives.  

七、特定技術議題研究與分析報告  
g. Research and Analytical Reports on Specific Technical Topics  

依客戶需求針對特定技術議題進行研究分析，並提出具體評估結果與建議報告。  
Conduct focused research on specific technical topics and provide analytical reports, findings, and recommendations.  


### 顧問服務特性 | Consulting Approach  

本顧問服務以獨立、客觀及專業分析為原則，提供技術評估、風險分析及決策建議。除雙方另有書面約定外，顧問服務 **不** 包含實際軟體開發、系統建置、產品銷售或專案執行工作。   
The consulting engagement is conducted on an independent and impartial basis, focusing on technical assessment, risk analysis, and decision support. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, consulting services do **not** include software development, system implementation, product resale, or project execution activities.
